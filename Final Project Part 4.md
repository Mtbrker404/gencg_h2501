
# Final Project - Botanic Visage 04

After some reflection, I realized this started to become more and more like a mirror into a parallel fruity world, where you would see yourself made of fruit. So after some complications of not being able to add more fruits to the face due to overwhelming the visage. I decided to add hand tracking to the project using ml5.js. It is nothing too complicated but allows a 2nd layer of immersion where the hands are tracked. The palm is estimated and then 5 spring onions spawn to show the 5 fingers you have.

What I would do to improve ? 

First thing would be to include occlusion, as when we turn our head the parts of the face overlap and reduce the realism of the portrait.

The next would be to find a way to add more fruits to the face with the occlusion, as without it the face was ruined due to high density of un-occluded moving parts.
