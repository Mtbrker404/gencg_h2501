---- Week 07 - Faces & Parametric Generators ----

Pareidolia... why is it that every chance our brain gets to create a face it does ? Is it our fear of loneliness ? Or simply a habit of humanising things ? 

Maybe somethings are best left unknown. Eitherway, my most impactfull concept of pareidolia is seeing faces on cars everywhere I go, to the point where I can even imagine what kind of personality they would have. 

Below are 3 Iterations of me trying to represent that with the front of a car.

Interestingly, each of these 3 pareidolia concepts display different emotions and personalities. Even though the front of the car is the same. In the 1st image, the face displays embarrassment, in the 2nd it displays a neutral/bored expression and finally the last face clearly portrays happiness. This goes to show how complex this pareidolia concept is. Furthermore I must consider the possibility that other people could imagine other personalities from these faces, showing how subjective this concept is.
